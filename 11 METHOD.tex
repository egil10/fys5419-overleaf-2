\subsection{Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO)}

\[
\min_{x\in\{0,1\}^n} \left( qx^T\Sigma x-\mu^Tx+\lambda(1^Tx-B)^2 \right)
\]

where $q$ is risk tolerance, $B$ is budget/number of assets to pick, and $\lambda$ is a massive penalty factor to ensure the algorithm respects the budget.
