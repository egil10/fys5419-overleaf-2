\section{Conclusions and Perspectives}\label{section:conclusion}

\subsection{Main findings}

The four parameter sweeps and the $p=1$ landscape map on the canonical $n=16$, $K=4$ instance support six concrete observations.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item On the canonical $n=16$ instance at $p=3$, QAOA sits firmly in a ``vanity ratio'' regime: the scaled approximation ratio $r \approx 0.94$ is dominated by the large $E_{\text{worst}}$ contributed by the budget penalty $AK^2$, while the absolute energy is roughly $2847\times$ the brute-force optimum and the top-1 success probability is only about $10\times$ the uniform baseline $1/2^{16}$ (see \cref{fig:single-run}).
    \item QAOA's solution quality is essentially flat across three orders of magnitude in the risk-aversion parameter $\lambda$, so risk aversion is not the bottleneck on this instance.
    \item The budget-penalty calibration $A$ does not produce the expected monotone improvement; instead, the $A$-sweep is dominated by COBYLA restart variance at the fixed budget of ten random seeds.
    \item The median top-1 success probability collapses geometrically with system size, from $p_{\text{opt}} \approx 0.23$ at $n=4$ to $p_{\text{opt}} \approx 1.6 \times 10^{-4}$ at $n=16$ (\cref{fig:scaling}), tracking $1/\binom{n}{K}$ almost as if QAOA were sampling uniformly over feasible bitstrings.
    \item The depth sweep is non-monotonic --- the ratio peaks at $r \approx 0.99$ for $p=2$, drops to $0.93$ at $p=4$, and partially recovers at $p=5$ (\cref{fig:depth-sweep}) --- consistent with the $2p$-dimensional landscape exceeding what ten random COBYLA seeds can reliably explore at $p \geq 3$.
    \item At $p=1$ the variational surface $E(\boldsymbol\gamma, \boldsymbol\beta)$ resolves into a small number of broad basins separated by saddles (\cref{fig:landscape}), so ten random COBYLA restarts reliably locate the optimum; the non-monotone behaviour from $p \geq 3$ onwards in \cref{fig:depth-sweep} is therefore consistent with the $2p$-dimensional angle space outpacing a fixed restart budget rather than with a defect in the ansatz itself.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Method-level discussion}

The exercise has clear methodological merits. Statevector simulation produces a clean, noise-free picture of QAOA dynamics suited to pedagogy, and the modular pipeline $(\boldsymbol\mu, \boldsymbol\Sigma) \rightarrow Q \rightarrow (h, J, c) \rightarrow H_C$ is dataset-agnostic. The variational principle $E \geq E_0$ supplies a built-in correctness anchor, and brute-force enumeration at $n \leq 16$ gives unambiguous ground truth against which approximation ratios can be reported honestly rather than relative to a moving target \parencite{farhi2014qaoa}.

The drawbacks are equally honest. At $n \leq 16$ the problem is classically trivial: brute force terminates in roughly $30~\text{ms}$ at $n=16$, and the greedy-Sharpe and simulated-annealing baselines reach the true optimum at machine precision while running about $4000\times$ faster than QAOA. The penalty mixer is sensitive to $A$ in a way that is difficult to tune without enumerating $C(\boldsymbol x)$ --- precisely the work QAOA is meant to avoid. Ten random restarts are borderline at $p=3$ and clearly insufficient at higher depth, and the standard QAOA literature \parencite{zhou2018qaoa} prescribes warm-started angles that were not used here.

The honest framing is therefore that QAOA at this scale is valuable for the questions it lets us ask --- what $E(\boldsymbol\gamma, \boldsymbol\beta)$ looks like, how $r$ scales with $p$, and where adiabaticity breaks down --- rather than for answers it provides over Gurobi on 16-asset portfolios. No quantum advantage is claimed.

\subsection{Future directions}

Several concrete next steps would directly address the limitations identified above, and they form a natural sequence from immediate fixes to longer-term comparisons. The most immediate is warm-started angle initialisation near the adiabatic schedule rather than from random points \parencite{zhou2018qaoa}, which should restore monotonicity in the depth sweep; a companion fix is to re-run that sweep with fifty or more random seeds so that landscape difficulty can be disentangled from optimiser variance. A constraint-preserving XY-mixer with a Dicke initial state \parencite{mancilla2026constrained} would remove the $A$ hyperparameter entirely by restricting the search to the Hamming-weight-$K$ subspace, and extending to inequality budgets $\sum_i x_i \leq K_{\max}$ via slack variables \parencite{thomassin2025slack} is a natural follow-on once the equality case behaves well. Beyond the encoding, scaling past the $2^n$ statevector barrier looks feasible through circuit cutting \parencite{soloviev2025cutting}, which would open up problem sizes where the classical baselines are no longer trivial. Benchmarking against alternative quantum heuristics such as quantum annealing \parencite{morapakula2025annealing} would situate the results in a broader landscape, and a direct comparison against Gurobi or CPLEX MIP on identical instances would, finally, quantify the QAOA-vs-classical gap rather than merely assert it.
