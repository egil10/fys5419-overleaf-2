\subsection*{E. Background literature}

The reference list collected during this project covers several overlapping themes; the entries below are cited here so that they appear in the bibliography for audit.

\paragraph{Foundational quantum mechanics.}
The original EPR paper~\parencite{epr1935} sets the historical context for entanglement, which underpins the quantum interference mechanism exploited by QAOA. Early demonstrations of small-scale quantum computation on molecular spin systems~\parencite{gershenfeld_qc_molecules} mark the transition from thought experiment to physical implementation.

\paragraph{Quantum computing textbooks.}
General textbook references include the foundational \textcite{nielsen_chuang}, and the more recent treatments of \textcite{hidary_qc_applied,hundt_qc_programmers,scherer_math_qc,olivares_student_qc,rieffel1998introqc,loredo_learn_qc_python_ibm}. Quantum-machine-learning specific texts include \textcite{schuld_qml,conti_qml,pattanayak_qml_python}.

\paragraph{QAOA and the variational paradigm.}
The QAOA algorithm itself is due to \textcite{farhi2014qaoa}, with the depth/landscape analysis of \textcite{zhou2018qaoa} and the introductory exposition of \textcite{giovagnoli_qaoa_intro,irfan_qaoa_simulators,ibm_qaoa_tutorial}. The closely related variational quantum eigensolver framework is reviewed in \textcite{tilly2022vqe}.

\paragraph{Many-body solvable models.}
The Lipkin--Meshkov--Glick model~\parencite{lipkin1965} provides a small, exactly solvable many-body Hamiltonian routinely used as a VQE benchmark in the FYS5419 course.

\paragraph{Markowitz and quantum portfolio optimisation.}
Modern portfolio theory originates with \textcite{markowitz1952}; statistical-learning context for return estimation is found in \textcite{hastie}. Recent QAOA- and annealing-based approaches to constrained portfolio selection are surveyed in \parencite{knapsack2024qaoa,thomassin2025slack,mancilla2026constrained,stopfer2025benchmark,shen2025noisy,morapakula2025annealing,soloviev2025pauli,soloviev2025cutting}.

\paragraph{Notation and standards.}
Mathematical notation conventions follow \parencite{iso80000_2}.

\paragraph{Software libraries.}
The implementations consulted while developing the code base include \parencite{qiskit,pennylane,qutip,qutip_github,pyscf,qbraid}.
